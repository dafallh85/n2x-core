N2X Core Whitepaper
Introduction

N2X Core is a decentralized Web3 infrastructure platform designed to connect blockchain technology, artificial intelligence, and smart digital networks into a unified ecosystem. The project provides a scalable foundation for next-generation decentralized applications, digital services, and intelligent automation.

The N2X token serves as the ecosystem's utility asset, enabling transactions, platform services, community participation, and future ecosystem expansion.

Vision

To become a leading decentralized infrastructure platform that empowers developers, businesses, and communities through intelligent Web3 technologies and AI-driven innovation.

Mission

N2X Core is committed to:

Building secure and scalable decentralized infrastructure.
Integrating Artificial Intelligence into blockchain applications.
Supporting Web3 innovation and digital transformation.
Creating an open ecosystem driven by community participation.
Delivering transparent and sustainable long-term growth.
