# N2X Core Whitepaper

## Introduction

N2X Core is a blockchain-focused ecosystem developing toward a decentralized Web3 infrastructure platform, exploring the intersection of blockchain technology, Web3, smart networks, and artificial intelligence.

The project is focused on developing future-ready infrastructure, digital services, and intelligent technologies.

The N2X token is designed as the utility asset of the N2X Core ecosystem. Current utility is focused on the project's initial ecosystem, community, development, and market activities. Additional platform services and ecosystem utilities are planned for future development.

## Vision

To become a leading decentralized infrastructure platform that empowers developers, businesses, and communities through intelligent Web3 technologies and AI-driven innovation.

## Mission

N2X Core is committed to:

- Building secure and scalable decentralized infrastructure.
- Integrating Artificial Intelligence into blockchain applications.
- Supporting Web3 innovation and digital transformation.
- Creating an open ecosystem driven by community participation.
- Delivering transparent and sustainable long-term growth.
